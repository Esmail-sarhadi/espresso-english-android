Features

Everything you need to hear it, get it and keep it

One app, six tools — all in a Persian, right-to-left interface.

Synced lessons

The audio and the text move together, line for line. Tap any sentence to stop right there and play it again.

Line-by-line translation

Under every English line, a plain Persian translation. You don't lose the meaning, and you don't lose the thread of the sentence.

Spaced review

Words come back at calculated intervals — exactly when you're close to forgetting them, not sooner.

Pronunciation practice

Read the sentence out loud and get a score. The audio is processed on the phone and never sent anywhere.

Synced lyrics

It recognises whatever song is playing and scrolls the lyrics with the music, with a translation for every line.

Translation bubble

A small bubble over any app. Tap it: the English on screen is read and translated in a clean panel.

Lyrics with the music

Every song is a listening lesson

The app recognises what's playing, finds the lyrics and scrolls them in step with the track. Every line carries its own Persian translation — no pausing the music, no searching, no losing the rhythm.

  • Playback detected on the phone itself
  • Scrolls with the beat, not with a timer
  • A Persian translation under every line
  • Works with any Android player

FAQ

What people usually ask

If your answer isn't here, ask on Telegram.

Is installing from outside Bazaar a problem?

No. Download the APK from this page. Android shows a security warning during the install; in settings, turn on “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager and tap install again. The app is signed, and it also installs from Bazaar and Myket.

Do I need the internet?

Not for the lessons. The audio, text, translations, glossary and the neural reading voice are all inside the app. You only need a connection for a new version or for the optional links.

How much space does it take?

The full build is about 240 MB and the ARM build about 137 MB, because the audio and the speech model are inside the package. After installing, you can move the app to a memory card.

Does my data go anywhere?

No. There's no sign-up, no account is created, and the app sends no network request for tracking. Your study stats, reviews and settings are stored only on the phone, and deleting the app deletes them.

Which Android versions does it run on?

Android 7.0 and up. On an old or 32-bit phone the ARM build is lighter; if you're not sure, install the full build.

How do I uninstall it?

Like any other app: hold the icon and tap Uninstall, or go to Settings › Apps › Espresso English › Uninstall. Deleting the app also clears the lessons and the local stats.
